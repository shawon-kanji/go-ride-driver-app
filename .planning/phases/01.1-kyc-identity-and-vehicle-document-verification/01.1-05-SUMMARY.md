## Accomplishments
- `DocumentTile` renders a distinct Badge for each of the four document states (not-uploaded/in-review/approved/rejected), shows the rejection reason inline with an explicit "No reason provided" fallback, and stays pressable in every state including approved
- `ReuploadConfirmDialog` names the document type and warns "reviewed again" before a driver replaces an already-approved document, delegating all rendering to the shared `ConfirmDialog` primitive
- Both components are pure presentational (no data fetching, no navigation, no local state) — ready to be composed into the Verify hub and capture screen in plan 01.1-06
