## Manual-Only Verifications

| Behavior | Requirement | Why Manual | Test Instructions |
|----------|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Real camera capture → real presigned PUT to AIStor/S3 → real confirm against a running local backend, using an actual document photo, for all 10 document types | KYC-01, KYC-02 | Presigned-URL signature correctness, real camera EXIF/orientation quirks, and real network upload timing cannot be exercised by mocks — Jest coverage proves client-side logic is correct in isolation only | 1) Run local `go-ride-backend` + AIStor via `make up`. 2) Build/run the app on a physical Android device via `npx expo run:android` (no emulator installed on this machine). 3) Log in as a test driver. 4) For each of the 10 document types, capture via camera and confirm the document appears with `status: uploaded` in `GET /driver/kyc/status`. 5) Manually flip one document's `status` to `rejected` with a `rejection_reason` via SQL, confirm the app shows the reason and allows re-upload. 6) Manually approve all 5 identity + all 5 vehicle documents via SQL, confirm `kyc_status` flips and the blocked-state banner disappears. |
| Blocked-state banner on real `403 KYC_NOT_APPROVED` / `403 VEHICLE_NOT_VERIFIED` from `PATCH /driver/online` and `POST /vehicles/{id}/activate` | KYC-04 | Requires the real backend gate (already implemented server-side) plus a driver in a genuinely incomplete KYC state — not worth mocking two distinct 403 codes end-to-end when the real backend is available and cheap to hit | With a test driver whose KYC is `not_started` or `in_review`, attempt to activate a vehicle and (once a vehicle is active) toggle online; confirm both produce the phase's specific blocked-state banner/dialog with correct copy and a working CTA into the Verify tab, not a generic error toast |
